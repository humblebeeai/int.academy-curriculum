# Draft Curriculum Overview

This is the proposed curriculum for review. Requirements may change; use the [Current curriculum](/docs) if you need the established learning path.

## Start here

1. [Engineering Fundamentals](/draft/engineering-fundamentals): confirm entry readiness, learn developer workflows, and build mathematical and data competence.
2. [Soft Landing Core Systems](/draft/softlanding/core-systems): complete six required modules, choose two electives, and deliver a usable AI service.
3. [Specializations](/draft/softlanding/specializations): continue into domain-specific work and a capstone. These pages retain the existing requirements.

## Study and demonstrate

Resources support practice. Keep runnable code, evaluation results, explanations, and evidence of responding to review. Follow module-specific completion requirements.

## Workload

Engineering Fundamentals estimates supplied in the guide are provisional. Expanded modules and Core Systems have workload estimates pending pilot. Agree a realistic study allocation with your cohort or mentor before setting a calendar.
