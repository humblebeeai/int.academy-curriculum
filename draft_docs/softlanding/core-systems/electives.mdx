# Electives Choose Two

Complete two of the five electives below. Each elective has its own practical outcome. An optional resource inside a required module does not count as one of your two electives.

- E1. Retrieval and RAG — find relevant information and produce grounded answers.
- E2. Agents and Tool-Based Workflows — complete defined tasks through tools and bounded orchestration.
- E3. Model Adaptation and Experimentation — adapt a pretrained model and compare experiments.
- E4. Backend Architecture and Data Systems — persistent data, background jobs, and caching.
- E5. Delivery, Monitoring, and MLOps — automated releases, observability, artifacts, and recovery.

## Choosing a combination

Choose based on a capability you want to develop and a project where you can practice it. Examples: E1 + E2 for retrieval-based workflows; E3 + E5 for model adaptation and delivery; E4 + E5 for backend reliability. Other combinations are equally valid when your project supports them.

## Relationship to specialization

These electives provide an introduction to a deeper area before specialization. Keep the scope manageable: one clearly defined feature, one evaluation approach, and an explanation of tradeoffs.

## Shared project, separate outcomes

You may implement both electives as extensions to your integrated project. Identify the work and evidence for each elective separately. One generic demonstration does not establish two different sets of learning outcomes.
