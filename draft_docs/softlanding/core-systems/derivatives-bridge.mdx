# Derivatives and Gradient Descent Bridge

## Purpose and prerequisites

Prepare for R3 after Engineering Fundamentals algebra, linear algebra, and R2. Derivatives are taught here; they are not required to enter Core Systems.

## Required learning and resources

Use selected [Khan Academy differential calculus](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/differential-calculus) lessons on derivative intuition, the power rule, and the chain rule. Study slope as a local rate of change, partial-derivative intuition, gradients, and the update direction in gradient descent. Formal proofs and a complete calculus course are not required.

## Practice

For a scalar squared-error function, compare a small finite-difference slope with the derivative at several points. Apply a few gradient-descent updates and plot the loss. Explain how an overly large step can increase loss. For a two-parameter example, explain why a gradient has one component per parameter. Revisit this example with PyTorch autograd in R3.

Workload estimate pending pilot. Include practice, debugging, review, and revision in your study time.

## Submission evidence and completion checks

Explain your approach and diagnose a relevant failure during review. Revise incomplete evidence after feedback; resource completion alone does not establish competence.
