## Our Mission

HumbleBeeAI exists to cultivate elite-level ethical AI talent and help humanity make the Earth a better place for everyone. We do that by democratizing access to real AI engineering competence.

This open curriculum is one of the clearest expressions of that mission: a structured, role-aligned pathway built from free and open resources, designed to take learners from "I do not know what to learn next" to "I can ship AI projects and prove it."

---

## What This Curriculum Is

- A **structured curriculum map** with clear progression and sequencing
- A **role-based pathway** from fundamentals to entry-level, role-aligned competence
- A set of **learning outcomes and proof artifacts** at every stage
- A **contributor framework** for adding modules, projects, and case studies

### What This Curriculum Is Not

- A claim that watching content makes you job-ready
- A replacement for practice, building, and iteration
- The HumbleBeeAI Academy program (the Academy is a separate, mentorship-driven layer on top of this open curriculum)

### Why Structure Matters

The internet already has strong courses and materials. What most learners lack is a coherent sequence and a way to measure progress with real outputs. Without structure, common patterns emerge: completing courses without a clear next step, learning tools before fundamentals, building disconnected projects, and accumulating knowledge without producing reviewable evidence of capability.

This curriculum defines **what** to learn, **in what order**, **to what depth**, and **what evidence** to produce at each stage.

---

## Who This Is For

**Learners:**
- High school graduates preparing for engineering-oriented AI roles
- Undergraduate students who want a clear, industry-aligned progression beyond coursework
- Career switchers who need a structured plan with measurable milestones and portfolio-grade outputs

**Contributors:**
- Experienced practitioners (5+ years) who want to build something useful for others
- Ability to curate open resources responsibly and write clear outcomes, exercises, and project specs

---

## Target Outcomes

This curriculum aims to help learners develop **entry-level, role-aligned competence** in one of the following roles: Data Scientist, Computer Vision Engineer, NLP/LLM Engineer, GenAI Engineer, or AI Software Engineer.

A learner demonstrates entry-level competence when they can:

- **Deliver role-aligned work end-to-end** — from problem framing to implementation and results
- **Explain key technical decisions** clearly and defensibly
- **Evaluate models correctly** using appropriate metrics, baselines, and error analysis
- **Produce reproducible results** with runnable repositories and clear setup instructions
- **Write practical documentation** that communicates approach, results, and limitations

---

## Curriculum Architecture

The default progression follows a structured path:

1. **Engineering Fundamentals** (recommended: 4 months) — Terminal, Git, math, Python, data handling
2. **Soft Landing** (recommended: 6 months):
   - **Core Systems** — shared engineering foundations for all roles
   - **Specialization Tracks** — role-based depth leading to entry-level readiness

Time estimates are for focused study. Part-time learners should expect longer. Learners with strong CS fundamentals may progress faster. The key is not speed — the key is consistency in learning and building.

This curriculum is **free to use**, open-source under CC BY-SA 4.0, and designed to be contributed to by experienced practitioners.
