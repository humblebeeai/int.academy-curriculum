# Soft Landing

## Purpose and prerequisites

Soft Landing connects engineering foundations to specialization. Complete [Engineering Fundamentals](/docs/engineering-fundamentals) or demonstrate equivalent skills through the Academy direct-entry review.

You should be able to write and debug Python, manage a local environment, collaborate with Git, work with NumPy and Pandas, query basic SQL, and interpret plots and foundational mathematics. Derivatives are taught in the deep-learning preparation section before deep learning.

## Stage 1 Core Systems

Complete [five required modules](/docs/softlanding/core-systems), [exactly two electives](/docs/softlanding/core-systems#electives), and an [integrated AI service project](/docs/softlanding/core-systems/handover#project). Keep evidence of implementation, evaluation, failure diagnosis, and review revisions.

## Stage 2 Specialization

Continue into a [specialization track](/docs/softlanding/specializations) and its capstone. Core Systems electives introduce capabilities explored more deeply in specialization.

## Workload

Workload estimate pending pilot. Include practice, debugging, review, and revision in your study time.
