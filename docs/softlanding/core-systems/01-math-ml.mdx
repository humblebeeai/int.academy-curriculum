# <Calculator /> Math & ML Foundations



## <Lightbulb /> What You'll Build Here

Machine learning isn't magic; it's just math (mostly multiplication and derivatives) scaling up.

This module peels back the layers of libraries like PyTorch. You will understand **how a single neuron thinks**, how it learns from mistakes (gradients), and why we structure data as vectors and matrices.

:::tip 💭 Real Talk
You can invoke `model.fit()` without knowing this. But you cannot **debug** a model that refuses to learn, or optimize an architecture, without speaking the language of Linear Algebra.
:::

## <BookOpen /> 1. Mathematics: The Language of AI

**Before diving into code, you need the language.** The focus here is on **intuition**, not manual calculation. You won't be solving 100 exercises on paper; you'll be building mental models of high-dimensional space.

Not every problem needs a billion-parameter Transformer. In fact, for tabular data, these "old school" models often win on speed and interpretability.

<ResourceCard

Production systems love Random Forests. They are robust, don't need scaled data, and are easy to explain to a boss. "The model failed because feature X was > 5" is easier to sell than "Matrix multiplication said so."
:::
