## <Target /> How to Choose?

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="interest" label="By Interest" default>

**Ask yourself:**

1. **"I love seeing my code interact with the physical world."** -> **Computer Vision**
2. **"I'm fascinated by how ChatGPT works and want to build my own."** -> **NLP & LLMs**
3. **"I want to create images, audio, and video with AI."** -> **Generative AI**
4. **"I love finding patterns in chaos and predicting the future."** -> **Data Science**
5. **"I care about performance, scalability, and building robust systems."** -> **AI Software Engineering**

</TabItem>

<TabItem value="market" label="By Job Market">

**Current Market Trends (2025):**

- **Highest Demand Volume:** AI Software Engineering (Everyone needs infrastructure)
- **Highest Hype/Growth:** NLP & Generative AI (Every company wants an LLM/GenAI strategy)
- **Most Stable:** Data Science (Every business needs analytics)
- **Niche High Pay:** Computer Vision (Robotics, Medical Imaging, Specialized Tech)

## <Rocket /> The Capstone

No matter which track you choose, you will finish the Specialization stage with a **Capstone Project**.

- **Duration:** 4-6 weeks
- **Scope:** Solving a real-world problem from end-to-end
- **Standard:** Production-grade quality (deployed, documented, tested)

This capstone will be the "crown jewel" of your portfolio—the thing you talk about for 20 minutes in every interview.
