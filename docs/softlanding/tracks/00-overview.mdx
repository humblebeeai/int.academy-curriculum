# 🎯 Choosing Your Specialization

![Choosing a specialization](/img/specialization.png)

At HumblebeeAI Academy, we believe in **T-Shaped Engineers**: broad general knowledge (Core Systems) and deep expertise in one specific area (Specialization).

## How to Choose?

This is a critical decision in your career. Do not choose based on "hype". Choose based on what you enjoy debugging for 10 hours straight.

:::tip Mentor Guidance
You are not alone in this decision. **Your Mentor will advise you** on which track fits your strengths and career goals best.
:::

## The 4 Tracks

### 👁️ [Computer Vision](./01-computer-vision.mdx)
**"I want to teach machines to see."**
*   **Best for**: Visual thinkers, matrix math lovers.
*   **Key Tech**: CNNs, YOLO, OpenCV, PyTorch.

### 📊 [Data Science](./02-data-science.mdx)
**"I want to find truth in numbers."**
*   **Best for**: Statisticians, detectives, analytical minds.
*   **Key Tech**: Pandas, Scikit-Learn, Hypothesis Testing, Visualization.

### 🤖 [Natural Language Processing](./03-nlp.mdx)
**"I want to build intelligent agents."**
*   **Best for**: Linguists, logic puzzle solvers, future AGI builders.
*   **Key Tech**: Transformers, LLMs (GPT), LangChain, Hugging Face.

### 🏗️ [Software Engineering](./04-software-engineering.mdx)
**"I want to build the systems that run the world."**
*   **Best for**: Architects, system designers, builders.
*   **Key Tech**: Distributed Systems, APIs, Docker, Cloud, Mobile.
