Specialization Tracks

Choose Your Weapon​

You have mastered the core systems. You understand the math, the models, and the infrastructure. Now it's time to go deep.

In the Specialization stage, you stop being a generalist and start becoming a Subject Matter Expert (SME).

The Five Tracks​

Computer Vision (CV)​

The Eye of AI

Teach machines to "see" the world. From analyzing medical X-rays to self-driving cars.

You Will Build:

  • Object Detection Systems (YOLO)
  • Image Classification Pipelines
  • Visual Recognition Systems
  • Video Analysis Tools

Best For:

  • Visual thinkers
  • Interest in robotics/hardware
  • Strong linear algebra skills

Explore CV Track →

NLP & LLMs​

The Voice of AI

Master language models, chatbots, and text analysis. Build RAG systems and fine-tune LLMs.

You Will Build:

  • Custom Chatbots with RAG
  • Fine-tuned LLMs for specific tasks
  • Multi-agent AI Systems
  • Semantic Search Engines

Best For:

  • Interest in language/linguistics
  • Passion for LLM applications
  • Strong logic/reasoning skills

Explore NLP Track →

Generative AI​

The Creator of AI

Build systems that generate content—images, audio, and video. Master diffusion models and speech technologies.

You Will Build:

  • Image Generation with Stable Diffusion
  • Speech-to-Text (ASR) Pipelines
  • Text-to-Speech (TTS) Systems
  • Video Translation Tools

Best For:

  • Interest in creative AI applications
  • Passion for multimodal systems
  • Want to work with cutting-edge generative tech

Explore Generative AI Track →

Data Science​

The Brain of Business

Turn raw data into actionable insights. The intersection of statistics, coding, and business strategy.

You Will Build:

  • Predictive Churn Models
  • A/B Testing Frameworks
  • Interactive Dashboards
  • Recommendation Engines

Best For:

  • Analytical thinkers
  • Business-minded engineers
  • Strong statistics foundation

Explore Data Science Track →

AI Software Engineering​

The Spine of AI

Build the infrastructure that makes AI possible. Scale models to millions of users.

You Will Build:

  • Distributed Training Clusters
  • Model Serving APIs (High Scale)
  • Feature Stores
  • Automated CI/CD Pipelines for ML

Best For:

  • Systems thinkers
  • Interest in backend/cloud
  • "I like code more than math"

Explore SWE Track →

The Capstone​

No matter which track you choose, you will finish the Specialization stage with a Capstone Project.

  • Duration: 4-6 weeks
  • Scope: Solving a real-world problem from end-to-end
  • Standard: Production-grade quality (deployed, documented, tested)

This capstone will be the "crown jewel" of your portfolio—the thing you talk about for 20 minutes in every interview.
