Curriculum Overview

Follow the learning path below, complete the required modules and electives, and keep your practical work ready for review.

Watch the roadmap introduction on YouTube

Start here​

  1. Engineering Fundamentals: complete three modules covering developer tools, Math for AI, and data foundations, then complete two electives.
  2. Soft Landing Core Systems: complete five modules, choose two electives, and deliver a usable AI service.
  3. Specializations: continue into domain-specific work and a capstone.

Study and demonstrate​

Resources support practice. Keep runnable code, evaluation results, explanations, and evidence of responding to review. Follow module-specific completion requirements.

Workload​

Engineering Fundamentals estimates supplied in the guide are provisional. Expanded modules and Core Systems have workload estimates pending pilot. Agree a realistic study allocation with your cohort or mentor before setting a calendar.

Feedback and community​

Use the contributors guide to suggest corrections or join the AI Roadmap Community.
