# 🤖 Module 5: IoT & AI Activation

![IoT and AI Activation - Robots and Sensors](/img/iot.png)

**Goal**: Connect code to the physical world.

#### 1. Teachable Machine & IoT
*   **Resources**:
    *   **[Google Teachable Machine](https://teachablemachine.withgoogle.com/)**
        *   **Why it's important**: Quick prototyping. See AI in action immediately.
        *   **What to expect**: You will train a model to recognize your own face/voice in 5 minutes.
    *   **Raspberry Pi / Simple IoT**
        *   **Why it's important**: AI often lives on edge devices (cameras, robots), not just big servers.
        *   **What to expect**: You will deploy your model to a small device to detect things in the real world.
