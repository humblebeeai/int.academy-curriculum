### Our Mentorship Philosophy (Action Over Instruction)

HumbleBeeAI Academy operates on the belief that **real expertise is built through action, not instruction**. Our learning model is **mentor-guided** and **project-based**, immersing learners in solving real problems from the start.

Mentorship is designed to provide a **personalized, mentor-driven environment** where every learner receives tailored feedback from experienced industry professionals who supervise a **small group of mentees**. This approach ensures students **receive tailored feedback**, build a credible **professional portfolio**, and develop **discipline and teamwork skills** essential for success in real AI teams.
