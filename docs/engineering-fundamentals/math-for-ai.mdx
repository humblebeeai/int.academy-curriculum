<ResourceCard

| Topic | What to learn |
| --- | --- |
| Vectors and tensors | Shapes, addition, scaling, norms, dot products, distance, and cosine similarity |
| Matrices | Operations, multiplication, transpose, identity/diagonal/symmetric matrices, transformations, and compatible shapes |
| Linear systems | `Ax = b`, inverse, rank, independence, pseudoinverse, and exact versus least-squares solutions |
| SVD and PCA | Eigenvectors/eigenvalues, eigendecomposition, SVD, low-rank approximation, and PCA |
| AI computations | Samples/features, batches, weight matrices, embeddings, attention shapes, broadcasting, and dimension errors |

Practise with NumPy and small hand calculations. Focus on understanding and application; formal proofs, abstract vector-space theory, change of basis, Gram–Schmidt, detailed subspace theory, large hand calculations, and decomposition derivations are outside scope.

**Submit four small notebooks:**

1. Vectors, norms, dot products, and similarity.
2. Matrix transformations and shape compatibility.
3. Linear systems and least squares.
4. SVD, low-rank approximation, and PCA.

Include an AI tensor-shape exercise and repair a dimension error. These are verification exercises, not an integrated project.
